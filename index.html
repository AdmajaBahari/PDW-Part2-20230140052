ABOUT NARUTO

When Masashi Kishimoto first created the Naruto series, he looked to other shōnen manga for inspiration when creating a unique character. Kishimoto cited Akira Toriyama's series Dragon Ball as one of his inspirations. Goku, the main character in Dragon Ball, was a major factor in the creation of Naruto Uzumaki due to his energetic and mischievous personality.
